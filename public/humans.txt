/* FUNDING */

	AIFitnessAPI is funded by KinesteX, an AI motion SDK. Pages that
	substantively cover KinesteX say so on the page itself and are flagged
	first_party in /answers.json. KinesteX is never called the best option
	here, and its claims are held to the same sourcing bar as everyone
	else's.

/* HOW CLAIMS ARE CHECKED */

	Facts trace to a primary source checked on the page's review date.
	Anything we could not confirm is labelled as unverified rather than
	rounded up, and dated claims carry their date. The full method:
	https://aifitnessapi.com/methodology

/* STACK */

	Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, MDX for posts.
	Content lives in typed data modules, so the HTML, the markdown mirrors,
	the feeds and the sitemap are all generated from the same source.
	Deployed on Vercel.

/* MACHINE-READABLE */

	/llms.txt, /llms-full.txt, /answers.json, /changes.xml, /feed.xml,
	/feed.json, /feeds/<cluster>.xml, /sitemap.xml
	Any page in markdown: append .md to its URL.
