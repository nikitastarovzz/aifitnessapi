## Item 4 decisions

### (a) Generated reference pages: the rule

`scripts/build-index-demand.mjs` writes `src/data/indexDemand.ts`; run it
after every pull, between two builds (the page list comes from the build). A
generated per-item page becomes `noindex, follow` and leaves the sitemap when
all three hold:

- zero Google and zero Bing impressions in every pull whose window ends on or
  after its first publication (the two engines are never added; the
  2026-10-02 snapshot has no Bing data and counts for Google only);
- first published at least 60 days before the newest pull's end;
- at least two pulls cover it.

Families judged: /healthkit-identifiers, /bluetooth-fitness,
/wear-os-data-types, /google-health-api data types, /health-connect records,
/workout-types activity pages, /pose-keypoints schemes and /libraries
packages. Hubs, topic pages and list pages (the four workout-type platform
lists, the Health Connect permission and aggregate tables, /healthkit/<group>,
/healthkit-versions/<ios-N>) are never judged. First publication comes from
`src/data/published.ts`, else from the date the file already holds (a ledger),
else on the first run from the commit that added the family's route, else the
run date; every fallback errs young. A short snapshot, a missing build or a
family below its floor fails the run. qa holds the result with
INDEX-DEMAND-READ, -ROBOTS, -SITEMAP and -HUB.

**Today it noindexes nothing.** The oldest per-item routes went live on
2026-10-03 (/health-connect, /libraries) and the rest on 2026-10-17/18, all
inside 60 days of 2026-10-24. Of 320 pages judged, 191 have impressions in
their pulls and 129 are unseen but too young: HealthKit identifiers 72 of 127,
Bluetooth 26 of 44, Wear OS 18 of 25, Google Health API 5 of 44, Health
Connect 3 of 42, workout types 3 of 9, pose keypoints 1 of 9, libraries 1 of
20. Counting every covering pull rather than one window is why more pages show
impressions here than in the Data section above. The first unseen pages become
eligible with a pull ending 2026-12-02 (Health Connect, libraries) and
2026-12-16 (the October 17 families).

### (b) Hand-written pages published before 2026-09-10 with no Google impression in this pull

72 pages (recomputed from `src/data/published.ts` and
`data/gsc/snapshots/2026-10-27.json`). Judged in this order, and the first rule
that applies decides:

1. **Keep** if Bing showed it in this pull. A page with impressions is not
   folded or noindexed (9).
2. **Keep** if it was first published after 2026-08-25, under 60 days at the
   pull's end, the same age bar as (a): too young to call (15).
3. **Keep** if Google showed it in the previous pull (2026-09-22 to 10-19);
   one empty window after a shown one is noise (6).
4. **Fold** only where a sibling owns the topic and the page holds nothing
   that sibling lacks (1).
5. **Noindex** otherwise (40). These serve readers but not search. None
   was folded: each of the plausible pairs (add-rep-counting and
   how-rep-counting-works, webhooks-vs-polling and what-are-webhooks,
   measuring-retention and engagement-metrics-that-matter, streaks and
   gamification) differs in format or carries sections the target lacks, and
   moving them would mean moving claims last checked in July and August.
   When unsure, noindex.

One judgement call outside the rules: /alternatives/strava-api-alternatives
is kept. It is the commercial format that earns clicks here, Google and Bing
both showed it earlier, and item 2 routes the bare "strava api" query this
round. It is judged with the rest at the 2026-11-10 pull.

Noindexed pages are `NOINDEX_PAGES` in `src/data/noindex.ts`. They stay live,
stay in their hubs, llms.txt, the markdown mirrors and site search, and keep
their links (`follow`). qa holds them with NOINDEX-PAGE-ROBOTS, -SITEMAP, -HUB
and -GONE (a folded page must leave the list). The fold is a 308 in
`next.config.ts` for the page and its .md address. A blog post has no release
list and no evidence rows, and nothing in corrections, changes, datasets,
the API, the MCP data or the package data named it.
