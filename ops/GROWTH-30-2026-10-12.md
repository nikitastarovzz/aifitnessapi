  now appeared in Google, up from 286 a week earlier.

- **Pages that repeat other pages don't surface.** Median share of a page's
  body text found verbatim elsewhere on the site: /questions 75%, /apis 70%,
  /health-connect 63%. Pages at 60–80% shared text: 32% never shown, 3.3
  average impressions. Pages at 20–40%: 11% never shown, 41.3 average.
- **The money pages now carry the stale flag.** Sixteen of the top pages,
  including ten /alternatives pages, were last verified 95–96 days ago, past the site's own 90-day flag. They took 52 of 165 page-level clicks
  (about a third).
- **US desktop distorts every click-through number.** It is 39% of
  impressions (5,748) and earned 15 clicks (0.26%). Outside the US, desktop
  clicks at 1.6% and mobile at 2.4%. The daily playbook's leak rule (≥30
  impressions, under 1% CTR) flags 62 of the 92 pages it applies to.
- **Google withholds the queries behind 95% of clicks** (8 of 162 are
  visible). **Bing reports the query behind 64 of its 66 clicks**, and 58% of
  its reported impressions are queries of six or more words.
- **Bing indexes broadly but sees no links.** Its index grew from 120 pages
  in early September to 681 (Oct 6). It reports zero inbound links. Its largest query
  theme is the Fitbit Web API turndown (307 impressions). The bare domain now
  takes most Bing page impressions (166 vs 128 for www in the latest week).
- **The site's own data collectors are dark.** Signups, page feedback,
  on-site search misses and real-user speed all write to Firestore, and the
  Firebase variables have been missing from Vercel Production since Oct 2.
  Every signup attempt shows the visitor an error.
- **No rich results.** Search Console's search-appearance report is empty
  for the whole period, despite the structured data on every page.
