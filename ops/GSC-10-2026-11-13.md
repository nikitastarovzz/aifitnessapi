- **Clicks keep growing; one day is suspect.** Nov 9 had 35 clicks, 10 of
  them on /pricing/exercise-database-api-pricing from 61 impressions, mostly
  US desktop. A 16% one-day CTR on a position-8 page is a person or a team,
  not search demand; read the week without it.
- **Google has nearly stopped indexing new pages.** Of 42 pages first
  published in November (src/data/published.ts), 2 have an impression;
  of 22 published Oct 27, 4; of 8 published Oct 22, 2. Before that, 75-90%
  of each half-month's pages drew impressions. None of /alternatives/rook-
  alternatives, /alternatives/sahha-alternatives, /fix/fitbit-api-410-gone
  (Nov 2), /graveyard, /research or /integrate/your-move-exercise-api has an
  impression. The pages are live, 200, `index, follow`, canonical to
  themselves and in the sitemap, which Google last read Nov 10 (971 URLs).
- **Our own pages started competing.** /motion/mediapipe-pose-landmarker-
  models, the page with the most impressions on the site in the MORE-30 read,
  went from about 30 impressions a day (Oct 16-30) to none from Nov 1, the day
  /apis/mediapipe first drew impressions (about 4 a day at 10.6). Over
  Oct 30-Nov 12 the /apis entries for WHOOP (106 impressions at 10.6),
  HealthKit (65 at 12.3), Polar, Fitbit, Health Connect and Oura ranked 7-17
  while their /integrate guides lost 25-50% of their impressions against
  Oct 16-29.
- **The "fitness api" owner is a page Google does not show.** /fitness-apis
  (owner since 2026-11-05) drew 27 Google impressions in three months; the
  homepage, also titled "Fitness API Guide: …", drew 1,232 at 27.9 and moved
  16.3 → 10.9 between the two halves of the window. Bing does rank
  /fitness-apis (#4, 5 clicks).
- **The exercise searches moved pages.** /fitness-apis/exercise-database-apis
  fell from 131 impressions to 8 while /pricing/exercise-database-api-pricing
  rose 307 → 565 and earned 21 clicks, the most on the site. "exercise api",
  "workout api" and "exercise database api" now show the pricing page at 20-27.
- **Much of the top-of-list volume is machines.** "tryrook.io alternatives"
  301 impressions at 2.1, "api fitness alternatives" 125 at 1.1, "best
  aggregator for wearable and sensor integrations" 133 at 4.4, "evaluate rook
  on health data api for developers" 86 at 8.1, "gym management software with
  open api and third-party integrations" 74 at 5.9: no click on any. A person
  shown a top-3 result clicks it at least sometimes; these read like AI answer
  engines grounding through Google. They make the pages look like CTR
  failures, and a retitle cannot win a click from them.
- **The Oct 27 retitle cohort did not lose to its titles.** As a group its
  impressions fell 25% while the rest of the site gained 30%, but page by page
  the "losses" are query mix: /integrate/healthkit ranks 44-98 for every query
  in both windows, /compliance/app-store-health-data-rules went from 9 to 93
  impressions (its average position fell because it now shows for more,
  deeper searches), /healthkit-errors gained impressions after its Nov 4 title.
- **Real searches with a page to earn:** "fitness app builder" 199 impressions
  in 90 days at 21 (/no-code-fitness-app), "gym gdpr compliance" 137 at 69
  (landing on the /compliance hub), "nutritics alternative" 25 at 37 (landing
  on the Nutritionix page, a different company).
- **The weekly pull is not running.** search-snapshot.yml run 7 (Nov 9)
  "succeeded" in seven seconds: it skipped for want of
  `GSC_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`. The last committed snapshot is 2026-11-03.
  Run locally, the pull was killed by a five-minute shell timeout during
  Bing's one-call-a-second page lookups, with no output to show it was alive.
