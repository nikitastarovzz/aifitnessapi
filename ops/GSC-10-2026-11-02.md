- **Clicks did not stall; they rose 34% week on week** and Oct 26–29 were the
  best four click days on record. What fell is impressions: from about 1,200 a
  day on Oct 22–26 to 700–800, the last two of those days a weekend and still
  provisional.
- **Most of the impression drop is demand, not rank.** Comparing Oct 20–25 with
  Oct 27–Nov 1: /fix/garmin-api-approval 252 → 83 at position 8.0 → 8.8,
  /compliance/app-store-guideline-5-1-3-health-research 188 → 41 at 6.2 → 6.1,
  /integrate/dexcom-api 88 → 8 at 5.8 → 6.3. Same rank, fewer searches — the
  Garmin program restructuring around Oct 22 was a spike that has passed.
- **Three real rank losses, all in the Oct 27 retitle cohort:**
  /integrate/oura-api 7.8 → 17.4, /devices/ant-plus-vs-bluetooth 10.2 → 17.0,
  /integrate/healthkit 14.0 → 24.3. Six days is noise for a retitle; SEO-10's
  measurement date is 2026-11-24. **Decision: hold**, do not retitle the
  cohort again before that read, or neither change can be measured.
- **None of the 13 pages shipped on Oct 31 has an impression yet** (/graveyard,
  /daily, /challenge, /2026, /play/ship-it, the five new tools, /market-map,
  /fitness-api-index). Google fetched sitemap.xml on Nov 1 (891 URLs). The
  last two waves have not reached search yet; their effect is ahead.
- **Top-10 pages that earn almost nothing** (Oct 19–Nov 1): garmin-api-approval
  426 impressions / 1 click, terra-alternatives 244 / 2 at 5.8,
  garminconnect-vs-garth 218 / 0 at 6.2, compliance 5.1.3 250 / 1 at 6.3,

  "evaluate rook on health data api for developers" and "… normalization" (53)

  "best wearable health data api for developers", "best aggregator for
  wearable and sensor integrations" (40, positions 8–9) land on

- **Today is the Fitbit Web API turn-off date.** Google's page (last updated
