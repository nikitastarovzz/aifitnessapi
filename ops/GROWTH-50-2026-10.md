# 50 changes — impressions and CTR, October 2026

Grounded in three things measured on 2026-10-02/03, not in general SEO advice:

- **`data/gsc/latest.json`** (2026-07-06 → 08-02, the only export that exists).
  141 pages, 4,191 impressions, 32 clicks, **0.76% CTR**. Against a
  position-aware curve, **38 pages rank in the top ten and have never been
  clicked**, across 416 impressions.
- **A live crawl of eight top-opportunity pages.** Titles, descriptions,
  schema and body depth are already good — 0 titles over 60 chars, 0
  descriptions outside 120–155 across all 276 entries, TechArticle + FAQPage +
  HowTo + Breadcrumb + Speakable present. The ordinary on-page checklist is
  done. What is left is structural.
- **`npm run qa`** and the query→page join, which surfaced the two findings
  below.

## The two findings this list is built on

**1. Cannibalisation. 44 of 234 queries are split across several of our own
pages — 450 of 1,220 query-page impressions.** "fitness api" is spread over
**13** of our URLs, all sitting between position 40 and 90. The recurring
diluters are listing and utility pages: `/site-index`, `/blog`, `/`, and
`/blog/how-to-choose-a-fitness-api` turn up against head terms that
`/fitness-apis` should own alone. Thirteen pages competing is why none of them
ranks.

**2. Forty-four pages have fewer than three inbound links** — and they are
precisely September's additions: 21 `/questions/*`, 12 `/healthkit/*`, 6
`/tools/*`. The largest new impression surface on the site shipped with almost
nothing pointing at it. The footer links roughly forty destinations and not one
of those forty-four.

Neither is a copywriting problem, which is why the previous pass could not see
them: it was looking at titles, and the titles were already fine.

## A. Consolidate what we are splitting (items 1–10)

Target: the 450 impressions currently spread across competing own-pages.

1. `noindex,follow` on `/site-index`. It averages position 156 across 102
   impressions and 0 clicks while competing on four head terms. It is a
   navigation aid, not a search destination; `follow` keeps its link equity
   flowing.
2. Same treatment for `/saved` and `/bookmarks`-style utility routes that hold
   no indexable content of their own.
3. Drop `/site-index` from `sitemap.xml` — a sitemap entry actively asks for
   the indexing we are removing.
4. Make `/fitness-apis` the unambiguous target for "fitness api" / "fitness
   apis" / "workout api": those exact strings belong in its h1, first
   paragraph and one h2, and nowhere else as a page's primary framing.
5. Re-point `/blog/how-to-choose-a-fitness-api` at its own long-tail intent
   ("how to choose") rather than the head term, and link it up to
   `/fitness-apis` as the canonical overview.
6. Add an explicit "this page covers X, for Y see Z" disambiguation line to
   the four worst-split clusters, so Google has a stated preference.
7. Audit `/`, `/blog` and `/guides` for head-term phrasing that makes them
   compete with the cluster hubs, and move it down a level.
8. Internal anchor-text discipline: links to `/fitness-apis` should use the
   head term; links to spokes should not.
9. `/fix` vs `/fix/fitbit-error-code-401`: the hub currently outranks its own
   spoke for "fitbit error code 401" (282 impressions, position 9.6, zero
   clicks). Rebalance so the spoke wins — it is the page that answers.
10. A `npm run qa` gate that fails when one query string is the stated
    `primaryQuery` of more than one released entry.

## Honest notes on this list

**Items 35, 37, 40, 48 and 49 shipped on 2026-10-02** and are listed for
completeness rather than counted as new work.

**Item 31 needs the owner** — a Bing Webmaster Tools property cannot be
verified from here.

**Nothing in section C is a guess about copy quality.** Each named page is one
the export shows ranking on page one and earning nothing. Where the export is
stale — it predates both the August CTR pass and September's 34 changes — the
item says what to change and why, and the `--compare` baseline pinned on
2026-10-02 is what will show whether it worked.

**What is deliberately not here:** another title-width pass (0 of 276 entries
are out of range), more schema types (the top pages already carry six),
and any new cluster. The site does not need more pages; it needs the pages it
has to stop competing with each other and to be reachable.
