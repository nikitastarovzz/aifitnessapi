## Why these three

From Search Console for 2026-09-12 to 2026-10-09 (`data/gsc/latest.json`),
Bing for the same window, and eight searches in the session's search tool:

- **Commercial, vendor-named pages earn the clicks.** Clicks per page:
  /alternatives 2.9, /pricing 1.6, /fitness-apis 1.0, /motion 0.8,
  /integrate 0.75. Reference sections earn close to nothing:
  /health-connect-api 125 impressions and 0 clicks; /build, /learn,
  /architecture and /data at 0.13 or below.
- **Vendor names are the biggest unmet demand.** Garmin alone is 48 queries
  and 336 impressions. The narrow Garmin queries rank 4 to 10, but "garmin
  api" sits at 43, "strava api" at 54, "whoop api" at 40 and "fitbit api" at
  70, and Google splits "garmin api" and "fitbit api" across two or three of
  our pages, the split SERP-30 #1 fixed for "fitness api".
- **Motion is the best non-commercial section** (1,373 impressions, average
  position 7.3). People search MediaPipe model-file URLs and "movenet vs
  mediapipe"; the competition is dev.to posts, student blogs and READMEs.
- **Long, assistant-style buyer questions already reach us near the top.**
  "Which wearable health data api is best for corporate wellness programs?"
  at 2; "a single api to pull all my participants' fitbit data" at 2.8;
  "build my own fitbit integration or use a research platform?" at 2.
